03 How it works

How Intellirity protects your AI.

You don't need to be a security engineer to use Intellirity. Turn on the Jailbreak & Prompt Injection Shield in three plain steps.

  1. 01 Connect

    Point us at your AI.

    Point Intellirity at the model, chatbot, or endpoint you already run. No code changes required. We sit in front of your AI and watch every request and response.

  2. 02 Configure

    Set your boundaries.

    Tell the system what is allowed. Our Jailbreak & Prompt Injection Shield blocks attempts to override instructions, steal your system prompt, or trick the model into leaking data.

  3. 03 Operate

    Watch it work.

    Every interaction is scored in real time. Suspicious activity returns a clear verdict: blocked, flagged, or safe, with a plain-language reason you can act on.
