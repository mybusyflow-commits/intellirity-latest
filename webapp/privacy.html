Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30 · This is a template and should be reviewed by counsel before publication.

Intellirity, Inc. ("Intellirity", "we", "us") provides AI security infrastructure. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to intellirity.com and the Intellirity control plane.

1. Information we collect

We collect account information you provide (name, work email, organization), configuration and policy data you create, and telemetry about product usage. When you run scans, we process the prompts, code, or URLs you submit solely to perform the requested analysis. We do not train general-purpose models on your submitted content.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, secure, and improve the Intellirity platform.
  • To detect and correlate threats across your environment.
  • To communicate with you about your account and product updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Data retention

Threat events and scan records are retained according to your plan (7–90+ days). You can export or delete data from your account at any time. Aggregated, de-identified metrics may be retained longer to improve detection.

4. Security

We apply encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access controls, and continuous monitoring. Our platform is aligned to SOC 2 and ISO 27001 controls.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, port, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact privacy@intellirity.com to exercise these rights.

6. Subprocessors

We use vetted infrastructure and analytics subprocessors. A current list is available on request.

7. Changes

We will post material changes here and update the "last updated" date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions: privacy@intellirity.com.
