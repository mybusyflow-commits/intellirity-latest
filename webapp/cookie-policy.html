Last updated: 2026-08-30 · This is a template and should be reviewed by counsel before publication.

This policy explains how Intellirity uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. When you first visit, a banner lets you Accept all or Reject non-essential cookies; your choice is remembered.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also use local storage and similar technologies for the same purposes.

2. Categories we use

CategoryPurposeCan be rejected?
Strictly necessaryAuthentication, security, load balancing, and remembering your consent choice.No: the site will not work without these.
FunctionalRemembering preferences such as layout and recent selections.Yes
AnalyticsUnderstanding aggregate usage so we can improve the product. Never used to identify you individually.Yes

3. Managing cookies

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser, after which the consent banner will reappear. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies in settings.

4. Third parties

We minimize third-party cookies. Where used (for example, font delivery), they are limited to what is needed to render the site.
